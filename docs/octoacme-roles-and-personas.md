# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder / Business Owner

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder or Business Owner defines the business need, approves scope and prioritization, and ensures the project delivers value to the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, assumptions, and expected outcomes
- Prioritize initiatives based on strategic value and urgency
- Approve scope, milestones, and trade-offs
- Provide decisions on funding, resourcing, and business constraints
- Validate that outcomes meet business needs and stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project work and organizational priorities
- Ensure clear accountability for business decisions

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone reviews with project and product leadership
- Business case and scope approval discussions
- Decision logs for trade-offs, priorities, and escalations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm value and priorities
- Collaborates with Project Managers on scope, timeline, and risk thresholds
- Provides direction to Developers and QA on business objectives and acceptance expectations
- Escalates strategic issues and major trade-offs to leadership when needed

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead ensures that the project delivers reliable, testable, and high-quality outcomes that meet agreed standards and business expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, test strategy, and validation approach
- Manage test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Partner with engineering to identify risks in quality, release, and regression coverage
- Monitor defects and ensure they are resolved before release
- Define exit criteria for quality sign-off

### Goals
- Reduce defects and rework
- Increase confidence in release readiness and customer experience
- Improve quality consistency across delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and risk discussions
- Quality status reporting for stakeholders and project leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to review testability and quality requirements
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release readiness and risk impact
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user impact
- Provides escalation when quality or risk thresholds are not met

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Architect provides technical direction, standards, and design guidance so the solution is scalable, maintainable, and aligned with enterprise principles.

### Responsibilities
- Define system design, architecture principles, and technical standards
- Review major design decisions and trade-offs
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and constraints
- Ensure alignment with security, reliability, and operational requirements
- Guide implementation decisions for complex or cross-team work

### Goals
- Maintain solution integrity and technical coherence
- Reduce architectural drift and avoid costly rework
- Support long-term scalability and maintainability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical risk assessments and dependency planning
- Cross-team design alignment and governance decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers with design guidance and technical constraints
- Works with Project Managers on delivery risk, dependencies, and sequencing
- Coordinates with Product Managers on feasibility and trade-offs
- Partners with QA on testing strategy for complex technical changes

---

## Risk Manager / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Risk Manager or Compliance Officer identifies, evaluates, and tracks project risks, regulatory obligations, and operational concerns that could affect delivery or business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain a risk register and evaluate impacts, likelihood, and response plans
- Monitor regulatory, legal, security, and operational compliance requirements
- Escalate critical risks and unresolved issues to leadership
- Support mitigation activities and contingency planning
- Ensure dependencies and governance requirements are documented and reviewed

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to delivery, compliance, and operational risk
- Maintain project resilience and governance quality
- Improve issue transparency and response speed

### Typical Communication
- Risk review meetings and escalation briefings
- Compliance checks and audit preparation
- Status updates during project reviews and critical milestones

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to track and mitigate delivery risks
- Works with Product Managers and Stakeholders on business impact and prioritization
- Provides input to Developers and Architects on security, compliance, and operational concerns
- Helps QA identify release risks and exit criteria

---

## Resource Manager / Capacity Planner

### Role Summary
The Resource Manager or Capacity Planner ensures the right people are available at the right time and that work is aligned with team capacity and organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Forecast capacity and staffing needs across initiatives
- Allocate people based on skill mix, availability, and urgency
- Identify bottlenecks, workload imbalances, and dependency conflicts
- Coordinate staffing decisions with leadership and project teams
- Support schedule adjustments when capacity constraints emerge

### Goals
- Maximize delivery efficiency and throughput
- Prevent burnout and scheduling conflicts
- Maintain alignment between demand and team capacity

### Typical Communication
- Capacity and staffing reviews
- Dependency briefings and resourcing decisions
- Planning updates with project and functional leaders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers on schedule realism and staffing assumptions
- Aligns with Product Managers on priority trade-offs when resources are constrained
- Works with Team Leads and Developers to understand workload and skill requirements
- Supports Stakeholders with resourcing impacts and trade-off guidance

---

## Communications Lead / Project Communications Manager

### Role Summary
The Communications Lead ensures stakeholders receive timely, relevant, and accurate information throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define communication plans and stakeholder audiences
- Coordinate status updates, leadership briefings, and escalation messaging
- Maintain transparency around progress, blockers, decisions, and risks
- Ensure messages are tailored to business, technical, and executive audiences
- Support change communication and adoption readiness

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder awareness and alignment
- Reduce confusion and misalignment across teams
- Support better decision-making through clear project communication

### Typical Communication
- Weekly project updates and milestone communications
- Stakeholder briefings, retrospectives, and launch communications
- Decision summaries and escalation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on cadence and reporting
- Works with Product Managers on messaging tied to value and roadmap milestones
- Supports Stakeholders and leadership in understanding project outcomes and trade-offs
- Coordinates with QA and Technical Leads when release or issue communication requires clarity

---

## Vendor / Contractor Manager

### Role Summary
The Vendor or Contractor Manager oversees external contributors, service providers, and partner teams to ensure delivery quality, compliance, and accountability.

### Responsibilities
- Manage vendor onboarding, contracts, and scope alignment
- Track performance, deliverables, and service quality
- Coordinate external dependencies and handoffs with internal teams
- Monitor vendor compliance with governance, contracts, and security expectations
- Escalate issues that affect delivery or business obligations

### Goals
- Ensure external work is aligned to project goals and quality expectations
- Minimize delivery risk from third-party dependencies
- Maintain accountability across partner organizations

### Typical Communication
- Vendor checkpoints and governance reviews
- Contract and deliverable reviews
- Dependency and escalation discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on external dependencies and timelines
- Coordinates with Product Managers on vendor deliverables tied to roadmap outcomes
- Partners with Architects and Developers to validate technical alignment
- Supports QA and Risk functions on compliance and quality oversight

---

## Role Interaction Summary

These personas complement the existing developer, product manager, and project manager roles by clarifying accountability across the broader project ecosystem. The goal is to ensure each function has a clear purpose, set of responsibilities, and points of collaboration with other roles. This improves decision-making, reduces ambiguity, and supports more consistent project execution across teams.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a prompt for Copilot Spaces to guide role-specific behavior and collaboration patterns.
- Teams can map responsibilities to a RACI-style matrix to clarify ownership across the project lifecycle.
